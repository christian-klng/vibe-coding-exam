## What is this about?
A **wireframe** is a rough sketch of a page: boxes, labels, no colors. It shows **what** goes where – not **how pretty** it looks. That comes later with **design**.

## Why does it matter?
A picture tells the AI more than a thousand words. Many AI tools can read sketches directly and build a first interface from them. And you consciously decide what gets space on your most important page.

## How to do it
1. Sketch your home page – on paper (photo), with [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) or as text.
2. Label the areas: navigation, main content, buttons …
3. Save the file as `docs/wireframe.png` (or `.svg`, `.excalidraw`, `.md`).
4. Embed it in `docs/sitemap.md`, e.g. with `![Home page](wireframe.png)`.

## Tip: How to ask Claude Code
> “Here is my wireframe (docs/wireframe.png). Describe what you see and ask where something is unclear.”
