## What is this about?
So far your app only runs on your computer (`localhost`). **Deployment** puts it on a server on the internet – with a **hosting** provider such as Vercel, Railway or Netlify. Afterwards it has a public address anyone can open.

## Why does it matter?
Only when real people use your app do you get real feedback. Many providers connect directly to your GitHub repo: every push to `main` publishes the new version automatically.

## How to do it
1. Choose a provider that fits your tech stack (see `docs/tech-stack.md`).
2. Connect your GitHub repo and start the first deployment.
3. Enter your environment variables with the provider (not in the repo!).
4. Open the address and check that everything works.
5. Add the URL to your README.
