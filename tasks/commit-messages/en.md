## What is this about?
A **commit** is a saved snapshot of your project – like a save point in a game. Every commit has a **commit message** describing what changed. Together they form the **version history**.

## Why does it matter?
When vibe coding, the AI often changes many files at once. When something breaks, you want to go back to exactly the last working state. That only works with small commits and messages that tell you what happened when.

## How to do it
1. Commit after each small, working step – not just at the end of the day.
2. Write **what** changed: “Add login form” instead of “update”.
3. Stick to one style, e.g. always imperative (“add”, “fix”).

## Tip: How to ask Claude Code
> “Look at my current changes, split them into sensible small commits and suggest a clear commit message for each.”
