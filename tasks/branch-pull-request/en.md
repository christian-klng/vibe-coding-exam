## What is this about?
A **branch** is a side track of your project: you can try out new things without risking the working version on `main`. A **pull request** (PR) is the request to bring the changes back into `main` – this is called a **merge**.

## Why does it matter?
Practically every software team works this way. A PR shows all changes clearly and is the place for feedback – from teammates or from an AI reviewing your code. If an experiment fails, you simply delete the branch.
