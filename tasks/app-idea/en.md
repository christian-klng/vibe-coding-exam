## What is this about?
Before you let the AI build anything, you need to know **what** should be built. A **value proposition** describes in one sentence which problem your app solves for whom – for example: *“StudyPlan helps students split their exam preparation into small daily goals.”*

## Why does it matter?
An AI builds exactly what you describe – even if it is vague. A clear sentence keeps your app from growing in all directions and becomes the starting point of every prompt.

## How to do it
1. Create a folder `docs` in your repo and a file `idea.md` inside it.
2. Answer: Which problem? For whom? How does the app help?
3. Sum it all up in **one** sentence.
4. Optional: write down what the app deliberately should **not** do.

## Tip: How to ask Claude Code
> “Ask me questions about my app idea one at a time until you know the problem, audience and benefit. Then write the result to docs/idea.md and sum it up in one sentence.”
